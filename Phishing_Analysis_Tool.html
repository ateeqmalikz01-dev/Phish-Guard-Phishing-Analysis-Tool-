Employee Red-Flag Checklist

The classic reference list every employee should run through before clicking anything. Each item maps to an automated detection in PhishGuard.

Identity & Sender

  • Display name mismatch: does the sender name say "PayPal" but the email address is @gmail.com?
  • Reply-To differs: pressing Reply silently sends your message to an attacker domain.
  • Spoofed brand: big-brand sender from a free mail / look-alike domain.
  • Generic greeting: "Dear customer / valued member" instead of your name.

Links & URLs

  • Hover before click: does the visible text match the real link target?
  • Typosquatting: paypa1.com, micr0soft.com, amaz0n.net.
  • Brand in subdomain: paypal.com.evil-site.xyz is NOT paypal.com.
  • IP address links, punycode (xn--), or @ inside the URL.
  • URL shorteners: bit.ly, tinyurl.com hide the real destination.
  • Abused TLDs: .tk, .ml, .ga, .top, .xyz, .click and similar.

Urgency & Pressure

  • "URGENT", "ACT NOW", "within 24 hours", "final notice".
  • Threats of suspension, legal action, fines or arrest.
  • Account will be deleted / locked unless you act immediately.
  • Scarcity: "last chance", "limited offer", "expires today".

Requests & Attachments

  • Asking for credentials, OTP, PIN, SSN, bank or card details. No legit service does this by email.
  • Unrequested attachments: invoices, receipts, .zip, .exe, .scr, "enable macros".
  • Payments by gift card / wire / crypto are a classic fraud signal.
  • Unexpected prize, lottery, inheritance or "you won".

Content & Style

  • Poor grammar, odd spelling ("verifiy", "acount"), all-caps emphasis.
  • HTML entities or obfuscated characters to hide the real address.
  • Hidden/zero-width characters inside the message.
  • When in doubt, verify through the official app/website, never the email link.

What to do when you spot a phish

  • Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
  • Report it via the Report Phishing button in your mail client.
  • Forward the full message to your security team (include raw headers).
  • Never enter credentials through an email-provided link.

How to use PhishGuard

PhishGuard is the detection phase of DecodeLabs Project 3 - Phishing Awareness Analysis.

1. Email Analyzer

  • Paste a raw email (headers + body) or just the body text.
  • Press Analyze Message.
  • Read the risk score, verdict, and every red flag with an explanation.
  • Use Export Report to print or save a report for your project portfolio.

2. Link Scanner

  • Paste URLs (one per line) to inspect them in isolation.
  • See the decoded URL, its parts, and each suspicious technique.

3. Red Flag Checklist

  • Reference sheet matching the project's goal: "List red flags found in phishing messages".
  • Every analyzed message also produces an automated checklist.

4. Scoring model

  • Each detected indicator adds risk weight. Total is capped at 100.
  • 0-15 Safe · 16-35 Low · 36-55 Medium · 56-75 High · 76-100 Critical.
  • A single critical indicator (e.g. IP-based login link + credential request) escalates the verdict.
  • Combinations of weak signals (e.g. urgency + URL shortener) are boosted, because they co-occur in real attacks.

Privacy

  • 100% offline - no data leaves your machine.
  • No external network calls are made.
  • Built as a single HTML file - open it in any modern browser.
